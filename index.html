About the author

Matthew Gunnoe

Matthew Gunnoe writes fast-moving thrillers and character-driven stories about ordinary people pushed into extraordinary situations. His work blends action, suspense, family, loyalty, and the consequences that follow when survival comes at a cost.

Twisted Skies Publishing is his independent publishing imprint and the home of the Derrick Anderson series.
